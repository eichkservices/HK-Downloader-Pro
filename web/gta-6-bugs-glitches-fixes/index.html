2. Why Xbox "Quick Resume" Causes Physics & Weather Glitches in GTA 6

On Xbox Series X and Series S, suspending GTA 6 in Quick Resume for multiple days can cause the in-game clock, dynamic tide simulation, and traffic spawning tables to desync—leading to flying boats in Lake Leonida or empty highways. Fix: Press the Xbox Guide button, highlight GTA 6, press Menu → Quit, and cold-boot the game before long sessions.

3. How to Clear PS5 Cache & Rebuild Database for Smoother Framerates

  1. Turn off your PS5 or PS5 Pro completely (wait for LEDs to stop blinking).
  2. Hold the Power button for 7 seconds until you hear the second beep to enter Safe Mode.
  3. Connect your DualSense controller via USB-C and select Option 6: Clear Cache and Rebuild Database. This takes 2 minutes, deletes zero game data, and eliminates shader/streaming stutter after big patches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are textures loading slowly in GTA 6 on my PS5?

If GTA 6 is installed on an M.2 expansion drive without a heatsink or with read speeds below 5,500 MB/s, the drive throttles during heavy Vice City streaming. Move the game to internal storage or use a 7,450 MB/s PCIe 4.0 SSD with a heatsink.

Are there any working money glitches in GTA 6?

Early launch builds had a pawn-shop buyback duplication glitch in Port Gellhorn, which Rockstar hotfixed in Title Update 1.02. Playing unpatched physical disc version 1.00 offline preserves launch-day physics and sandbox glitches.
