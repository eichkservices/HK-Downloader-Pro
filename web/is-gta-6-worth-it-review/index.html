3. Is It Worth Buying a PS5 Pro ($699) or Xbox Series X ($499) Just for GTA 6?

One of the most common questions on PC gaming subreddits (r/pcmasterrace and r/buildapc) is whether PC players should buy a console for GTA 6 or wait 12–18 months for the PC port:

  • When Buying a Console IS Worth It: If avoiding major plot spoilers for over a year is impossible for you, buying a PS5 Pro ($699) or a used/new PS5 Slim / Xbox Series X ($449–$499) lets you play Day One. Because consoles retain strong secondary market value, you can play GTA 6 for 12 months and resell the console + physical disc for ~75% of what you paid—making your net "rental" cost roughly $10/month.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is GTA 6 worth buying at full $69.99 price?

Yes. With over 60 hours of core narrative and 200+ hours of single-player open-world content across the state of Leonida—plus GTA Online access—it delivers more content per dollar than almost any AAA release of the decade.

Is the GTA 6 Deluxe or Collector’s Edition worth the extra money?

For 80% of players, the $69.99 Standard Edition has 100% of the story and map. The $99.99 Deluxe Edition is only worth it if you want the physical SteelBook case, printed Leonida poster map, and an early cash/vehicle head start in GTA Online.

Is it worth upgrading from a base PS5 to a PS5 Pro for GTA 6?

If you game on a large 55-inch+ 4K OLED TV (like the LG C4), the PS5 Pro’s PSSR AI upscaling and upgraded ray-tracing cores noticeably reduce shimmer on Vice City neon reflections and foliage compared to the base PS5.

How long is the GTA 6 main story?

The main story campaign featuring Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval takes approximately 55 to 70 hours to complete at a normal pace, and over 200 hours for 100% completion across Leonida.

Do you need to have played GTA 5 to understand GTA 6?

No. GTA 6 tells a standalone story set in Leonida and Vice City with brand-new protagonists (Lucia and Jason), though veteran players will recognize returning HD-universe brands and Easter eggs.
