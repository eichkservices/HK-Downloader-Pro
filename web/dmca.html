Last updated: October 15, 2026

HK Downloader Pro complies fully with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512) and international copyright standards. We respect intellectual property rights and respond promptly to legitimate notices of infringement.

1. Nature of the Service

HK Downloader Pro does not host, upload, re-encode, or store any copyright-protected media files on its servers. The service operates purely as a client-side and proxy delivery tool that converts URLs supplied by end users into streaming format parameters.

2. Filing a DMCA Notice

If you believe that your copyrighted work is being referenced or processed through our service without authorization, please submit a notice containing:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • The exact URL(s) of the material or content.
  • Your full name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is unauthorized.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
