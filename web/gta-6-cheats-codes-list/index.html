Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a money cheat code in GTA 6?

No. Like GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, Rockstar did not include a button cheat for instant money. To make fast money in Story Mode, rob armored cash transports, fence high-end jewelry in Port Gellhorn pawn shops, and complete heist finales.

Do GTA 6 cheats permanently ruin my save file?

Only if you overwrite your main save slot while a cheat is active. If you keep a separate clean save slot and reload it after playing with cheats, your Trophies and Achievements remain 100% intact.

Where do you type cheat phone numbers in GTA 6?

Press Up on the D-Pad to pull up Lucia or Jason’s smartphone, open the Contacts app, press Square (PS5) or X (Xbox) to open the numeric dial pad, enter the number, and press Square/X again to call.
