1. The Setting Is Not Just Vice City — It’s the Entire State of Leonida

One of the biggest misconceptions among casual players returning from GTA: Vice City (2002) or GTA V (2013) is that Grand Theft Auto VI is confined to a single city. In reality, Vice City is merely the metropolitan anchor of the fictional state of Leonida (Rockstar's satirical recreation of Florida).

Community cartographers from the GTA 6 Mapping Project cross-referenced trailer coordinates, highway signage, and telemetry data to confirm six distinct biomes across Leonida:

  • Vice City Metro & Vice Beach: Neon-lit Art Deco hotels along Ocean Drive, Starfish Island mansions, downtown skyscrapers, Venetian islands, and packed beaches.
  • Lake Leonida: A massive inland freshwater lake sitting at the center of the map (inspired by Lake Okeechobee), supporting high-speed boats, fishing, and dynamic water wakes.
  • The Grassrivers (Everglades Swamps): Dense mangrove wetlands patrolled by airboats, giant alligators, pythons, and off-grid smuggling syndicates.
  • Port Gellhorn: A gritty industrial port town and coastal strip on the western coast featuring pawn shops, motel holdouts, and street racing circuits.
  • The Leonida Keys: A long southern archipelago connected by sweeping overseas highway bridges (modeled after the Overseas Highway to Key West) with deep-sea diving and cartel yacht routes.
  • Ambrosia & Redhill Rural Heartland: Agricultural plains, mud-bogging tracks, county jails, and backwoods biker territory.

2. Lucia Caminos & Jason Duval: How Dual Protagonists Work

Unlike GTA V's chaotic three-way dynamic (Michael, Franklin, and Trevor), GTA 6 focuses on an intimate, high-stakes criminal partnership between Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval:

  • Solo & Joint Switching: You can switch between Lucia and Jason individually across the map, or bring both together to execute synchronized robberies where one controls crowd intimidation while the other cracks safes or drives the getaway car.
  • Lucia’s Ankle Monitor Early-Game Mechanic: Early in the story, Lucia is released on probation wearing an electronic GPS ankle monitor—restricting how far she can roam from Vice City until the narrative unlocks the full state of Leonida.

3. Gameplay Realism Changes Inspired by Red Dead Redemption 2

Before jumping in expecting GTA Online's flying rocket bikes, here are the grounded gameplay mechanics introduced in GTA 6:

  1. Limited On-Body Weapon Carry + Car Trunk Stash: You can no longer pull an RPG, minigun, and eight rifles out of thin air. Lucia and Jason carry sidearms concealed on their person plus a slung long gun or duffel bag, while your personal vehicle's trunk functions as your mobile weapon locker.
  2. Smarter Police AI & 6-Star Wanted System: Police no longer instantly teleport to your exact bush. Officers establish crime-scene perimeters, track your specific vehicle make/color and license plate (forcing you to ditch stolen cars or swap plates), and deploy tactical SWAT/Coast Guard units at higher wanted stars.
  3. 70%+ More Enterable Interiors: Supermarkets, pawn shops, strip malls, nightclubs, restaurants, motel rooms, and apartment blocks can be entered seamlessly without loading screens.
  4. In-Game Social Media Ecosystem: A fully simulated TikTok/Instagram-style vertical video feed inside your in-game smartphone broadcasts viral chaos from NPCs across Leonida—and reacts to your own high-profile crimes.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

GTA 6 stars dual protagonists Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval in a Bonnie-and-Clyde style crime story across Vice City and the state of Leonida.

How big is the GTA 6 map compared to GTA 5?

Community coordinate mapping estimates the State of Leonida is roughly 1.8x to 2x larger in total landmass than GTA V’s Los Santos and Blaine County, with dramatically higher urban density and hundreds of enterable building interiors.

Does GTA 6 have a separate GTA Online mode?

Yes. The next generation of GTA Online set in Leonida launches alongside GTA 6’s lifecycle, expanding session player counts, structured roleplay (RP) systems powered by Rockstar’s Cfx.re (FiveM) acquisition, and dynamic map updates.

Can you swim, surf, and explore underwater in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar built a brand-new real-time fluid physics system in RAGE 9 specifically for Leonida’s oceans, the Leonida Keys coral reefs, and the Grassrivers swamps.

Will GTA 6 work on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. GTA 6 requires a PlayStation 5, PlayStation 5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S, or a high-spec gaming PC (when the PC edition launches).
