15 Things You Need to Know Before You Get GTA 6 (Map, Gameplay & Changes)

Updated Nov 2026 • 3 min read • GTA 6 Series
⚡ Quick Read — Full Summary 30-Sec Overview

Don’t want to read the whole guide? Here are the biggest gameplay and map changes in GTA 6:

  • Massive State of Leonida Map: ~2x the landmass of GTA V, featuring Vice City, Lake Leonida, the Grassrivers swamps, Port Gellhorn, and the Leonida Keys.
  • Dual Protagonists (Lucia & Jason): Switch between them solo or team up in real time for synchronized heists and getaways.
  • Realistic Weapon Carry (Car Trunk Armory): No more pulling 10 rocket launchers out of thin air—heavy weapons are stored in your personal car’s trunk (like RDR2’s horse saddle).
  • 70%+ More Enterable Interiors: Walk directly into malls, pawn shops, nightclubs, motels, supermarkets, and restaurants without loading screens.
  • Overhauled 6-Star Police AI: Cops surround crime scenes, remember your vehicle model/plate, and force strategic escapes.

1. Map & World Changes: Welcome to the State of Leonida

GTA 6 expands far beyond Vice City into the full fictional state of Leonida. Community mapping confirms six distinct regions:

  • Vice City & Vice Beach: Neon Art Deco Ocean Drive, downtown high-rises, Starfish Island mansions, and packed beaches.
  • The Grassrivers (Everglades): Mangrove swamps filled with airboats, alligators, and smuggling routes.
  • The Leonida Keys & Port Gellhorn: A southern island chain connected by sweeping ocean bridges, plus an industrial west-coast port city and central Lake Leonida.
