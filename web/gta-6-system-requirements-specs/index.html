Frequently Asked Questions

Can an RTX 3060 or RTX 4060 run GTA 6?

Yes, at 1080p Medium-High settings with DLSS Quality enabled. The 12GB RTX 3060 handles high-res textures comfortably.

Is 16GB RAM enough for GTA 6?

16GB is the minimum for 1080p, while 32GB DDR5 is recommended for 1440p and 4K to avoid background hitching.

Does GTA 6 require an NVMe SSD?

Yes. Mechanical hard drives (HDDs) cannot stream Leonida’s world assets fast enough; a PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD is required.
