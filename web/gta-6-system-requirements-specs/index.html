To run GTA 6 on console, you need a PlayStation 5, PlayStation 5 Pro, or Xbox Series X|S (PS4 and Xbox One are unsupported). Base consoles target a rock-solid 30 FPS at upscaled 1440p–4K with mandatory ray-traced global illumination, while the PS5 Pro utilizes PSSR AI upscaling for superior 4K clarity and enhanced ray-traced reflections. For PC players building or upgrading for the PC release, you will need at minimum an 8-core CPU (Ryzen 5 5600X / Core i5-12400F), 16GB RAM, an 8GB–12GB RT-capable GPU (RTX 3060 Ti / RX 6700 XT), and a PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD—while 1440p/4K Ultra requires a Ryzen 7 7800X3D/9800X3D, 32GB DDR5, and an RTX 4080 Super / RTX 5080 (16GB VRAM).

  • Console Target: PS5 Pro (4K PSSR), PS5 / Series X (1440p-4K)
  • Minimum PC GPU: RTX 3060 12GB / Radeon RX 6700 XT
  • Recommended PC GPU: RTX 4070 Super 12GB / RX 7800 XT
  • 4K Ultra PC Target: RTX 4080 Super / 5080 + Ryzen 7 9800X3D

1. Why GTA 6 Is the Most Demanding Open-World Game Ever Built

Unlike cross-generational titles that had to scale down to 2013 Jaguar tablet CPUs and mechanical 5,400 RPM hard drives inside the PS4 and Xbox One, Grand Theft Auto VI was engineered from day one exclusively around current-gen Zen 2 / Zen 4/5 CPU architectures, hardware ray-tracing pipelines, and PCIe Gen 4 NVMe SSD streaming.

Technical analyses by graphics engineers and Digital Foundry highlight three specific pillars in RAGE 9 (Rockstar Advanced Game Engine 9) that dictate what hardware you need:

  • Always-On Ray-Traced Global Illumination (RTGI) & Reflections: Vice City's neon-lit Ocean Drive, car paint reflections, rear-view mirrors, and interior bounce lighting rely on hardware BVH ray-tracing traversal rather than static baked lightmaps.
  • Extreme CPU Simulation & Neural NPC Density: Trailer beaches and city blocks showcase over 80–120 unique NPCs simultaneously with individualized cloth physics, social-media animations, procedural body morphology, and reactive traffic AI. This places a massive load on CPU L3 cache and memory bandwidth.
  • Zero-Loading Interior/Exterior Asset Streaming: Flying a jet or driving a hypercar at 180 MPH across Leonida streams gigabytes of uncompressed 4K PBR textures per second directly from your NVMe SSD into VRAM.

4. Buying Guide: What Hardware Should You Upgrade First for GTA 6?

If you are upgrading your setup this year, prioritize your budget in this exact order to eliminate bottlenecks:

  1. Avoid 8GB VRAM GPUs for 1440p/4K: High-resolution PBR textures + ray-tracing BVH structures in RAGE 9 easily exceed 10.5 GB of VRAM at 1440p. Always choose a 12GB or 16GB VRAM graphics card (such as the RTX 4070 Super 12GB, RTX 4080 Super 16GB, or RX 7800 XT 16GB).
  2. Prioritize AMD 3D V-Cache CPUs (Ryzen 7 7800X3D / 9800X3D): Open-world Rockstar titles are famously CPU-bound in dense downtown areas. AMD's 96MB 3D V-Cache improves 1% low framerates by up to 28% in heavy crowd simulations.
  3. For Gaming Laptops, Check GPU TGP (Wattage): When buying a gaming laptop for GTA 6 (like the ASUS ROG Strix SCAR 16 or Lenovo Legion Pro 7i), pick an RTX 4080 Laptop GPU (175W TGP, 12GB VRAM) or higher alongside 32GB DDR5 RAM.
  4. Pair Your PS5 Pro or PC with an HDMI 2.1 120Hz VRR OLED Display: A 120Hz VRR display (like the LG C4 OLED) unlocks 40 FPS Balanced modes and Low Framerate Compensation (LFC), making console gameplay feel dramatically smoother than a standard 60Hz TV.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can an RTX 3060 or RTX 4060 run GTA 6?

Yes. An RTX 3060 (12GB) or RTX 4060 will run GTA 6 at 1080p Medium-High settings using DLSS Quality upscaling. The 12GB RTX 3060 actually holds a texture-headroom advantage over 8GB cards when ray-traced GI is active.

Why does GTA 6 run at 30 FPS on base PS5 and Xbox Series X?

GTA 6 is heavily CPU-bound due to its dense NPC AI simulation, vehicle physics, and mandatory ray-traced global illumination across Leonida. The Zen 2 CPU inside consoles prioritizes world simulation density at a locked 30 FPS (or 40 FPS VRR mode on supported displays).

Is 16GB of RAM enough for GTA 6 on PC?

16GB of dual-channel RAM is the minimum requirement for 1080p, while 32GB of DDR5 RAM is strongly recommended for 1440p and 4K to prevent background asset hitching.

What is the best gaming laptop for GTA 6?

Look for a laptop equipped with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 (12GB VRAM, 175W full TGP) or RTX 4090 (16GB VRAM), an Intel Core i9-14900HX or Ryzen 9 7945HX3D CPU, 32GB DDR5 RAM, and a 2TB PCIe 4.0 SSD, such as the Lenovo Legion Pro 7i or ASUS ROG Strix SCAR 16.

Does GTA 6 require an SSD?

Yes, 100%. Mechanical hard drives (HDDs) and slower SATA SSDs cannot keep up with RAGE 9’s continuous world-streaming pipeline. A PCIe Gen 4 NVMe M.2 SSD (5,000+ MB/s) is essential.
